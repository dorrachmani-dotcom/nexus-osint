One-time setup in Google Cloud (about 5 minutes)

  1. Open console.cloud.google.com and create a project (any name, e.g. "Nexus brief").
  2. Enable the Gmail API for that project.
  3. Open Google Auth Platform → Branding, fill in the app name and your email. Under Audience choose External and add yourself as a test user.
  4. Under Clients, create an OAuth client of type Desktop app. Copy its Client ID and Client secret into the fields below and save.
  5. Click Connect Gmail and approve "Send email on your behalf". Nexus can only send; it cannot read your mail.
  6. Stay connected: while the project is in Testing, Google expires the permission after 7 days. Under Audience click Publish app; Google then shows an "unverified app" notice on the consent screen (normal for an app you set up for yourself) and the connection lasts.
