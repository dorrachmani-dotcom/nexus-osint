Watchlists & alerts

Your silent tripwires. Every time the system scans for new items, it checks every incoming article, post, or report against your watchlist. The moment something matches, the item is flagged with a red ALERT tag — so you notice it instantly without reading everything yourself.

{# --- What you can monitor --- #}
🔍 Keyword

A word or phrase. Matches anywhere in the title, body, or AI summary — case-insensitive.

Examples:
"John Smith"
"money laundering"
"offshore account"

Best for: names of people, companies, places, or specific terms you need to catch immediately.

🔨 Regex

A pattern using regular expressions. Catches flexible formats — dates, codes, IDs — that a simple keyword would miss.

Examples:
\b\d{9}\b (9-digit ID)
passport\s*\d+
\+\d{7,15} (intl. phone)

Best for: document numbers, registration plates, email addresses, or any structured data.

💰 Crypto wallet

A full wallet address (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Tron, etc.). Flags any article that mentions it — even in a long list of addresses.

Examples:
1A1zP1eP5QGefi
2TWLZjsfseoFs9
0x742d35Cc6634
C0532925a3b844

Best for: following the money — tracking wallets linked to sanctioned entities, ransomware groups, or suspects.

📞 Phone number

A phone number in any common format. Recognised with or without country code, spaces, or dashes.

Examples:
+1-555-867-5309
(555) 123-4567
+44 20 7946 0958

Best for: numbers tied to suspects, shell companies, or known threat actors — surfaces every mention across sources.

One watchlist entry can trigger across any source: news, social media, or custom feeds.
